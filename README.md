### What I Care About

- **Developer Experience (DX):** I like reducing friction for engineers—clear interfaces, good documentation, and tools that “just work.”
- **Reliability and Observability:** Systems should be measurable and debuggable. I care about logs, metrics, traces, and good alerting.
- **Automation:** If a task is repeated often, I prefer to automate it and turn it into a reliable workflow or tool.
- **Scalability:** I think about how systems evolve over time—how they scale, how they are maintained, and how easy they are to extend.
